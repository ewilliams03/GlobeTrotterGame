About

A few minutes, somewhere new.

Globetrotter Games is a small collection of daily geography puzzles. Each one takes a few minutes and leaves you with something true and surprising about the world.

Six games, every day: Globe Links, Fact or Fib, Rank the World, Mystery Passport, Outline Drop and Flag Forge.

Why I made this

Every country has something that would surprise you: a festival, a dish, a word, a strange fact about its border. I built Globetrotter Games because I think learning those things makes us better neighbours to the rest of the world.

I believe global competency is one of the most valuable things anyone can build. It makes you more curious, more open-minded and better at getting along with people who aren't like you. Most of us just don't get many chances to practice it.

It's easy to live inside a bubble. Same feed, same places, same opinions. That isn't anyone's fault; it's what happens when the world feels far away. These games are my small attempt to change that: free, quick daily puzzles that let you step outside the bubble for a few minutes and leave you knowing a little more about the world than you did yesterday.

If a round made you smile, share it with a friend. Have an idea to make it better? Send me a recommendation.

Facts you can check

Every fact in a game links to a source. We aim to describe every country and culture accurately and respectfully, and we avoid disputed territories and political framings.

Free, and private by design

There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Your streaks are kept only in your own browser. The privacy page has the details.
