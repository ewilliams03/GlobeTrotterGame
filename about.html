About

A few minutes, somewhere new.

Globetrotter Games is a small collection of daily geography puzzles. Each one takes a few minutes and leaves you with something true and surprising about the world.

Six games, every day: Globe Links, Fact or Fib, Rank the World, Mystery Passport, Outline Drop and Flag Forge.

Why I made this

Hi, I'm Ethan. I believe being globally competent matters more than ever: knowing where places are, how people live there, what they eat, what they celebrate and what they care about. It's easy to stay inside our own bubble, surrounded by the same news, the same places and the same points of view. The world gets a lot bigger, and a lot friendlier, once you step outside it.

I built Globetrotter Games to help grow that global competency, one small daily trip at a time. A few minutes a day spent with a flag you've never seen, a country you couldn't place on a map or a fact that surprises you can widen your horizons more than you'd expect.

My hope is simple: the more we understand about each other, the less divided we feel. If these games spark a little curiosity about somewhere new, and a little more connection to the people who live there, they've done their job.

Have an idea to make it better? Send me a recommendation.

Facts you can check

Every fact in a game links to a source. We aim to describe every country and culture accurately and respectfully, and we avoid disputed territories and political framings.

Free, and private by design

There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Your streaks are kept only in your own browser. The privacy page has the details.
