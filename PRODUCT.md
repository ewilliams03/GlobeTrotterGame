# Globetrotter Games

register: brand (hub, about, privacy) · product (game screens)

## Product purpose
A free hub of short, daily geography games. Each game takes a few minutes and teaches something surprising about countries, cultures and the planet. Six games are planned: Globe Links (live), Fact or Fib (live), Rank the World (live), Mystery Passport (live), Outline Sketch (live), Flag Forge (live).

## Users
Curious people of every age (students, commuters, trivia fans) playing on their phone or laptop in a spare few minutes, often once a day. They play for the small win and the "huh, I didn't know that" moment.

## Scene
Someone on a bus or a couch, in daylight or under a warm lamp in the evening, thumbing through a three-minute puzzle with a cup of coffee. Mood: curious, a bit competitive, relaxed. Light theme by default; a true dark theme for evening use.

## Brand personality
Bright, confident, playful but never childish. Feels like modern transit wayfinding (clear signage, bold line colours, numbered routes) crossed with a well-made board game. Not sepia maps, not parchment, not compass roses, not neon.

## Anti-references
- Vintage explorer maps, parchment textures, serif "adventure" type
- Purple SaaS gradients, glass cards everywhere, hero-metric layouts
- Copies of other daily games' UI, colours, wording or names (NYT Connections, Wordle and friends)

## Principles
1. Zero data collection. No accounts, analytics, ads, cookies or third-party requests. Stats live only in the player's browser.
2. Facts are true and sourced. Neutral, respectful wording about every culture.
3. Every tap feels answered: instant press feedback, crisp motion, nothing sluggish.
4. Accessible by default: keyboard play, readable contrast, colour never the only signal, reduced motion respected.
