How to play

Find four groups of four countries that share something: a flag detail, a language, a quirk of the map.

Pick four tiles and press Check. A wrong guess costs one ticket, and you have five. Groups run from Easy to Expert.

Stuck? You get three free hints a day. Each one names a group you haven't found yet.

Each group you solve lights up its links on the map below. Turn on Flags only to hide the country names.
