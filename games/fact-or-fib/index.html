Fact or Fib

How to play

You'll visit five countries. Each comes with three statements: two are true and one is a fib. Pick the fib.

Then say how sure you are. Certain scores 2 if you're right and loses 1 if you're wrong. Hunch scores 1 if you're right and nothing if you're wrong.

You get three free hints a day. A hint rules out one of the true statements.
