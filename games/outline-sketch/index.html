Outline Drop

How to play

Three countries today, two steps each. First, pick the outline: four shapes, one is the country. Right first time is worth 50, second try 25.

Then drop it on the map: tap the blank world map where the country belongs. Tap again to move it, then lock it in. Anywhere on the country, or within 300 km of it, is worth the full 50; further off scores less.

You get three free hints a day: in step one a hint removes two wrong outlines; on the map it circles the right region.
